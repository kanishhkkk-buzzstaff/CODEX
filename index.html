Before you pay anyone

  1. You never need a PIN or OTP to receive money.
  2. Police, CBI and courts don't arrest anyone over video call. “Digital arrest” is a scam.
  3. Bank and telecom staff will never ask for your OTP or to install a screen-sharing app.
  4. An international number that claims to be an Indian government office is fake.

    Keep yourself aware सावधान रहें

    Scams change their story, but the pressure is always the same: hurry, fear, or a reward that's too good. These are the ones people in India report most.

    Six habits that stop most scams

    1. Hang up and call back on a number you find yourself, from the bank's card or official website.
    2. Never share OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or screen. No real employee needs them.
    3. Scanning a QR code or entering a PIN only sends money. It never receives it.
    4. Don't install apps like AnyDesk or TeamViewer, or APK files, because a caller asked.
    5. Pause when rushed. “Within 2 hours” and “don't tell anyone” are red flags.
    6. Check the number here, and report it so the next person is warned.
